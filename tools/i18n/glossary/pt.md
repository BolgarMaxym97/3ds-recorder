# Glossary: Portuguese (pt), Brazilian Portuguese (pt-BR)

What exists officially:

- **The console itself speaks Brazilian Portuguese.** On Americas-region 3DS consoles, System Settings >
  Other Settings > Language offers English, French, Spanish and Portuguese
  (https://en-americas-support.nintendo.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/233/). Brazilian players see these
  menus. NoA did not publish a Portuguese operations manual (its 3DS manuals are English, French and
  Spanish), but its Brazilian support site **pt-americas-support.nintendo.com** describes the 3DS
  menus step by step, label by label. It is the primary source below.
- **Nintendo of Europe** published European-Portuguese manuals (*consola*, *ecrã*, *Definições da
  Consola*, Title Case). They are used here only to cross-check. **Do not use them for the wording:**
  Brazilian players say *console*, *tela*, *configuração*, *baixar*, *salvar*.
- **Nintendo's later pt-BR material** (Switch, nintendo.com/pt-br) fills a few gaps.

## Conventions

- **Address: você.** Instructions are imperatives in the *você* form: *Toque em OK*, *Selecione…*,
  *Aperte e segure o botão POWER* (BR-region, BR-power). When a sentence needs a subject, it is *você*:
  "Se você quiser tentar substituir o sistema…" (BR-freeze). Never *tu* or *o senhor*.
- **On-screen buttons are infinitives or nouns:** *Abrir*, *Mover*, *Apagar*, *Aceitar*, *Configurar*,
  *Seguinte*, *OK* (BR-region, BR-sd, BR-transfer, BR-setup). A button says *Converter*; its help says
  *Toque em Converter…*.
- **Pressing a button: *aperte*, *aperte e segure*** (BR-power: "Aperte e segure o botão POWER por dez
  segundos"; BR-freeze). The guide uses *aperte* ×181 and *pressione* ×63. Both are fine; prefer *aperte*.
  Touch: *toque em* (all BR sources).
- **Confirm button: *OK*** (BR-region: "Selecione o país e toque em OK").
- **Capitalization: sentence case** for menu and button labels: *Configuração do console*, *Outras
  configurações*, *Gerenciador de dados*, *Controle dos pais*, *Configuração de internet* (BR-region,
  BR-sd, BR-internet). Running text: *o menu HOME* with a lowercase *menu* (×20 in the BR pages, never
  *Menu HOME*). EU-PT's Title Case (*Modo de Descanso*, *Cartão SD*) is European and is not used here.
  Keep *HOME*, *POWER*, *START*, *SELECT* as they are.
- **Quotation marks: “ ”** (BR-freeze: ‘começam com “CW” ou “CB”’; EU-PT too). Not « ».
- **Punctuation:** no space before *: ; ! ?*. Ellipsis *…*. Space between a number and its unit
  (*25-35 cm* in BR pages; *30 s*, *2 GB*). Decimal comma.
- **Gender:** *o console* (masculine, BR), so *o 3DS*, *o New 3DS*, *seu console* (BR pages; guide
  "Desligue seu console"). EU-PT *a consola* is European.
- **Brazil vs Portugal words** (always the Brazilian one): *tela* not *ecrã*; *baixar* not *transferir*
  (for download); *salvar* not *guardar*; *configuração* not *definição*; *celular* not *telemóvel*;
  *arquivo* not *ficheiro*; *aplicativo* not *aplicação*; *usuário* not *utilizador*; *controle* not
  *controlo*.

### Feature names (style rule 5), suggested

Recorder: *Gravador*. Replay (30 s): *Replay (30 s)* or *Repetição (30 s)*. Launch On Startup: *Iniciar ao ligar*.
Convert To MP4: *Converter para MP4*. Work With Lid Closed: *Funcionar com o console fechado*.
Share Statistics: *Compartilhar estatísticas*. Gallery: *Galeria*. Download: *Baixar*.
Settings: *Configurações*. Help: *Ajuda*. "Ready for download": *Pronto para baixar*.

## App terms (as translated)

Final wording in `app/source/lang/pt.c` and the phone page (`build/i18n/web-pt.js`). Labels (buttons, rows,
paths after →) are in sentence case as on screen; in running text a feature that is also a plain noun phrase
goes lowercase with its article (*o gravador*, *a repetição*, *o início automático*). A switch named as a
control in a sentence is quoted: *desative “Gravador” no painel*, *ative “Repetição 30 s”*. Address: *você*
imperatives everywhere (*aperte*, *toque em*, *abra*, *segure*).
