Primary sources (Nintendo of Europe, Italian):
- [3DS-IT] Nintendo 3DS manuale di istruzioni: https://www.nintendo.com/eu/media/downloads/support_1/nintendo_3ds_14/Nintendo3DS_OperationsManual_IT.pdf
- [3DSXL-IT] Nintendo 3DS XL manuale di istruzioni: https://www.nintendo.com/eu/media/downloads/support_1/nintendo_3ds_14/Nintendo3DSXL_OperationsManual_IT.pdf
- [N2DSXL-IT] New Nintendo 2DS XL manuale di istruzioni (New-generation system software): https://www.nintendo.com/eu/media/downloads/support_1/new_nintendo_3ds/NewNintendo2DSXL_OperationsManual_ITA.pdf
- [SD-IT] Nintendo support "Usare le schede SD": https://www.nintendo.com/it-it/Assistenza/Console-precedenti/Usare-le-schede-SD-826532.html
- [HG-IT] 3DS hacks guide, Italian translation: https://3ds.hacks.guide/it_IT/ (source: github.com/hacks-guide/Guide_3DS, `docs/it_IT`)

## Conventions

- **Address: tu on screen.** The manuals use the impersonal infinitive ("Toccare l'icona…", "Spegnere la console"), but the console's own messages use *tu*. The on-screen message quoted in [3DSXL-IT] and [N2DSXL-IT] reads: "Si è verificato un errore. Tieni premuto il pulsante POWER per spegnere la console. Per maggiori informazioni, consulta il manuale di istruzioni della console." The on-screen buttons are also tu imperatives (below). [HG-IT] uses tu ("Spegni la tua console"). Use **tu**. Never use Lei.
- **Buttons and menu items:** tu imperatives or nouns: CHIUDI, CONTINUA, SPEGNI (POWER menu), AVANTI, RICEVI DA UN NINTENDO 3DS, NON ADESSO, plus OK and the first-person ACCETTO [3DS-IT], [N2DSXL-IT]. Use the imperative for actions ("Salva", "Scarica", "Disinstalla", "Converti").
- **Capitalization:** sentence case on screen. The manuals print labels in CAPITALS only as typography. On screen: "Impostazioni della console", "Gestione dati", "Impostazioni generali", "Cancella impostazioni" [HG-IT], [3DS-IT]. "menu HOME" has a lowercase *menu* [3DS-IT].
- **Quotes:** Nintendo IT uses curly doubles “…”: cartella “Nintendo 3DS”, “Si è verificato un errore…” [3DS-IT], [3DSXL-IT]. «…» is also correct Italian, but follow Nintendo: “ ”.
- **Punctuation:** no space before `: ; ! ?`. Use the ellipsis `…`. Number and unit with a space ("2,4 GHz", "30 s"), decimal comma.
- **Gender:** *la console* (feminine), but a model is masculine: "RICEVI DA UN NINTENDO 3DS" [3DS-IT]. The community follows this: "un New 3DS", "un Old 3DS" [HG-IT].

## App terms (as translated)

Final wording in `app/source/lang/it.c` and the phone page (`build/i18n/web-it.js`). Address: *tu*; buttons are
imperatives; labels in sentence case. In running text a feature that is a plain noun phrase goes lowercase with its
article (*il registratore*, *il replay*, *l'avvio automatico*). A switch named as a control is quoted with “ ”:
*disattiva “Registratore” nel pannello*, *attiva “Replay 30 s”*.
