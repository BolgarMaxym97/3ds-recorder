# Glossary: Traditional Chinese (zh-Hant)

Sources used (all text-extracted and searched):

- **[X]** Nintendo 3DS XL 使用說明書, SPR-001(TWN). It is one manual for Taiwan and Hong Kong (it lists both the 臺灣用 and
  香港用 AC adapters): https://nintendo.com/hk/3ds/SPR_manual.pdf
- **[C]** Nintendo 3DS 使用說明書 (CTR, TWN): https://nintendo.com/hk/3ds/CTR_manual.pdf (same terms as [X])
- **[H]** Nintendo HK 3DS support page: https://nintendo.com/hk/3ds/support.html
- **[G]** 3DS Hacks Guide, Traditional Chinese translation (partly translated):
  https://github.com/hacks-guide/Guide_3DS/tree/master/docs/zh_TW

**Taiwan vs Hong Kong.** Nintendo sold one TWN-region 3DS with one Traditional Chinese system and one manual for both
markets ([X]). The official vocabulary is therefore one set, and it is Taiwanese: 軟體 (HK colloquial 軟件), 螢幕
(HK 屏幕), 網路 (HK 網絡), 資料 (HK 數據), 影片 (HK 視頻 / 影片). Even Nintendo HK's own page uses 軟體 ([H]).
This glossary uses Taiwan's wording throughout, which is also what HK 3DS owners see on their console.

## Conventions

- **Register.** Polite **您**: [X] has 您 77 times and 你 0 times (「以指示燈的顏色與閃爍，通知您主機的狀態。」).
  Requests use 請…: 「請從 HOME 選單的主機設定進行觸控螢幕校正。」.
- **Button and menu labels.** Short verbs or nouns, quoted in 「」 in running text. From [X]: 「確定」「開始」「下一步」「結束」
  「開啟」「關閉電源」「現在不設定」「同意」「設定」「讀取」.
- **Punctuation.** Full-width ，。、：（）！？, and **「」** for quoting labels and names (210 「 vs 4 “ in [X],
  where the 4 are in English licence text). Use ／ (full-width slash) between alternatives: 「開啟／關閉電源」,
  「調整螢幕亮度／省電模式」 ([X]). Use … (U+2026) for ellipsis.
- **Latin text and numbers.** [X] puts a **half-width space** between Latin words or numbers and Han characters:
  「HOME 選單」「SD 卡」「3D 螢幕」「DS/DSi 軟體」「Nintendo 3DS XL 主機」「26 頁」「5 秒鐘」. There is no space next to
  full-width punctuation. Keep this style (style.md rule 7 says "no spaces between words", which holds: the spaces
  sit only at Latin–Han boundaries).
- **Button names.** The manual writes 電源鍵, HOME 鍵 ("按主機的 HOME 鍵"). Use `A 鍵`, `L + Y`.
